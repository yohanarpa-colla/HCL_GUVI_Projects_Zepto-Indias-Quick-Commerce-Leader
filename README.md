# Zepto – Business Strategy Report Using Qualitative & Quantitative Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Business Strategy Analysis of Zepto**, one of India's leading Quick Commerce companies.

The analysis evaluates Zepto's **market position, business model, customer perception, technology, competitor landscape, SWOT factors, customer sentiment, and growth challenges**. Based on the findings, practical business recommendations are provided to support **customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, competitive advantage, and sustainable growth**.

---

## 🏢 Company Selected

**Company:** Zepto  
**Domain:** Quick Commerce  
**Founded:** 2021  
**Founders:** Aadit Palicha & Kaivalya Vohra  
**Headquarters:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze Zepto's position in the Indian Quick Commerce market.
- Understand Zepto's business model and revenue streams.
- Identify Zepto's unique selling propositions (USPs).
- Analyze customer needs and market gaps addressed by Quick Commerce.
- Study technology and innovation used by Zepto.
- Analyze customer reviews and classify sentiment as Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
- Identify common customer feedback themes and pain points.
- Benchmark Zepto against major competitors.
- Perform a SWOT analysis.
- Generate actionable business recommendations for sustainable growth.

## 🔍 Customer Review Methodology

### Data Sources

- Google Play Store reviews
- Public customer feedback
- Social media and online discussions
- Public market and business reports

### Review Sample

- **Sample:** 2,000 recent Google Play reviews
- **Period:** April 2026

### Preprocessing

- Removed irrelevant and spam reviews
- Grouped similar complaints into common themes
- Standardized sentiment categories
- Focused only on meaningful customer feedback

### Key Review Themes

**Positive**
- Fast delivery
- Product variety
- Competitive pricing
- Convenience

**Neutral**
- Delivery variations
- Product availability
- Offers and discounts

**Negative**
- Refund and customer support issues
- Delivery problems
- Product quality concerns
- Trust-related complaints

> Note: The review-level findings and recent rating statistics are specific to the analyzed sample and should not be interpreted as company-wide customer satisfaction metrics.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

The analysis identified the following major findings:

- Zepto has achieved strong growth and significant scale in the Indian Quick Commerce market.
- Speed and convenience remain major reasons customers use Quick Commerce.
- Technology and hyperlocal fulfilment are important competitive advantages.
- Customer support, refunds, delivery issues and trust are key areas requiring improvement.
- Zepto faces strong competition from Blinkit and other established and emerging players.
- Rapid growth needs to be balanced with profitability and operational efficiency.
- Advertising and category expansion provide opportunities for additional revenue growth.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### Short Term – 0–12 Months

- Provide faster refunds for eligible issues.
- Improve customer support response times.
- Send proactive delivery-delay notifications.
- Strengthen product-quality checks.
- Improve inventory accuracy.

### Long Term – 1–3 Years

- Improve unit economics.
- Expand the advertising business.
- Increase product categories and basket size.
- Selectively expand into Tier II and Tier III cities.
- Strengthen customer retention and loyalty.
